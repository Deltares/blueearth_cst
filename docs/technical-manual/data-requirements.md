Data requirements depend on the analyses you choose. Check that each source covers your basin and
study period and is suitable for the assessment question. Historical climate needs particular attention:
it drives the historical simulation and conditions weather generation, while the model is not locally
calibrated.

## Datasets

- **[Spatial data](spatial-datasets.md):** terrain, rivers, land cover and soils for building the basin model.
- **[Historical climate](historical-meteorological-data.md):** meteorological records for climate analysis,
  model forcing and weather generation.
- **[Climate projections](climate-projection-data.md):** model ensembles for interpreting the climate
  changes tested, independently of scenario generation.

Discharge observations, where available, support historical model evaluation.

## Data access and preparation

- **[Data catalogs and access](data-catalogs-and-access.md):** how to locate and read external datasets.
  The supplied starter requires Deltares P-drive access or a suitable alternative catalog.
- **[Input formats and conventions](data-formats-and-conventions.md):** how to supply observations and
  reporting locations, with consistent variables, units, calendars and spatial references.
